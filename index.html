About Me

I am a first-year M.S. student in Computer Science & Technology at Xi'an Jiaotong University. I received my B.S. from Yanshan University, ranked 1st out of 191. My research focuses on large language models and multimodal / omni-modal foundation models. I have interned at ByteDance Seed, TikTok, and iFLYTEK, working on omni-modal data and training optimization, large-scale multimodal content understanding, and medical LLMs.

My research interests include:

  • Multimodal & Omni-modal Foundation Models
  • Continued Pre-training, Mid-training & Post-training
  • Data Curation, Training Diagnostics & Efficient Multimodal Reasoning

I am actively seeking research collaborations in LLMs and multimodal / omni-modal intelligence. I am deeply enthusiastic about ambitious, high-impact research—if our interests align, please feel free to reach out.

Experience

Research Intern · ByteDance Seed
2026.02 – Present
Seed-Omni · Data & Training Optimization

Worked on data and training optimization for Seed-Omni, spanning continued pre-training and mid-training across speech, text, and vision. Built a three-stage data-governance pipeline combining rule-based filtering, LLM-assisted review, and modality validation, together with sample- and token-level diagnostics for tracing loss spikes, data conflicts, and capability regressions. Developed reusable production and synthesis pipelines for dialect speech, VS2T, audio captioning and QA, SpeechQA, and long-form interleaved data, improving key workflows by up to 50×. Took end-to-end ownership of a 12B/128K, 463B-token mid-training run—from data recipe and training configuration through monitoring, checkpoint selection, evaluation, and regression attribution—and distilled the findings into reusable team practices.

Research Intern · TikTok, ByteDance
2025.02 – 2025.09
EcoMLLM · Multimodal Content Understanding

Led EcoMLLM, a unified multimodal-LLM framework for large-scale short-video content understanding that replaced a stack of task-specific models. Designed a two-stage cascade—a multi-view VLM producing structured descriptions plus a compact LLM for fast downstream inference—and built Juggle Pipeline, a Ray + Merlin data system with 30+ operators for constructing large-scale instruction data. Combined training-free token pruning, FP8 quantization, and visual-token compression to increase QPS from 5.65 to 17.1 (>3×) without measurable downstream degradation. The system achieved 59.58% video-VQA accuracy and around 90% precision and recall on core tasks; RL (GRPO) with CoT cold-start and composite rewards improved US recall by 35% and instruction following to 100%.

Algorithm Intern · iFLYTEK
2024.10 – 2025.01
Medical LLM · SFT + DPO

Built an LLM assistant that converts doctors' speech into standardized ultrasound reports with evidence tracing and diagnostic reasoning. Designed a multi-level annotation framework, expanded 200 real clinical audio-report pairs to 1,000 with GPT-4o, and fine-tuned a 13B medical LLM with full-parameter SFT and DPO alignment. The system achieved an 80%+ qualified-report rate; the work led to a first-author national invention patent and was deployed to assist clinical diagnosis at a partner hospital.
